Wedding Games Hire FAQs

Straight answers about booking, payments, collection, weather, changes and what to expect on the wedding day.

Booking & payments

Booking, changes and payment.

How does booking work?

Start by checking your wedding date and venue postcode. If the date is available, choose mini golf, a games-only package, a combined package or Build Your Own. You’ll see the price, extras and any postcode-based travel fee before payment. A 25% deposit secures bookings made 30 or more days before the wedding, with the balance due 30 days before the date. Bookings made fewer than 30 days before the wedding must be paid in full.

Can I change my booking after I’ve paid?

Yes. You can add or remove games, change your selection, add or remove an Event Host, or adjust other booking details before the wedding, subject to availability. We do not charge amendment fees; you simply pay any difference in the items you choose. To make a change, email bookings@onthegreengames.co.uk and we’ll update the booking with you.

What’s your cancellation policy?

The 25% deposit is non-refundable. If you cancel 7 or more days before the wedding, any amount paid above the deposit is refundable. If you paid in full at booking, 25% of the total is treated as the non-refundable deposit. Cancellations made fewer than 7 days before the wedding are non-refundable.

Wedding-day arrangements

Delivery, collection and setup.

How long is the hire period?

Our packages are all-day hire rather than fixed two-hour or three-hour slots. Delivery and collection arrangements are agreed for your booking so the entertainment can fit around the wedding day.

When will you collect the games?

Collection timing is agreed with you on a case-by-case basis. It depends on factors such as your venue location, travel arrangements and the timings of your wedding. We’ll confirm the collection arrangements for your booking so you know what to expect before the day.

Do you set everything up?

Yes. We deliver, professionally set up and collect the mini golf course and giant garden games. You do not need to build or pack away the equipment yourselves.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain does not automatically stop the entertainment. Where practical, we can use a suitable indoor or covered setup. In heavy rain, high winds or storm conditions we may adapt the layout or delay setup until it is safe. In genuinely unsafe conditions, we’ll discuss the safest options with you rather than cancelling without warning.

Can the mini golf be set up indoors?

Yes, where your venue has a suitable, reasonably flat space and permits the equipment. The modular course can also work in marquees, on suitable terraces and outside on lawns.

Do you stay and run the games?

Every booking is self-serve as standard: we deliver and set up, then your guests play at their own pace. Where mini golf is included, you can add an Event Host for £400 to welcome guests and manage play.

Can children play?

Yes. Mini golf and the giant garden games are suitable for mixed-age wedding groups. Children should remain supervised by a responsible adult throughout the hire.

What if something gets damaged?

Normal wear and tear is never charged. If equipment is accidentally damaged beyond normal use, we charge fairly based on the actual repair or replacement cost.

Products & practical questions

What you can hire and where.

Can I hire mini golf on its own?

Yes. Mini Golf Only is £400. See everything included with Mini Golf →

Can I hire the giant garden games without mini golf?

Yes. Games-only packages are £200 for any 3 games, £325 for any 5, or £400 for all 7. See the Giant Games →

What’s included with mini golf hire?

The 9-hole course, putters, golf balls, scorecards, pencils, all-day hire, delivery, professional setup and collection are included. An Event Host is optional.

Which areas do you cover?

Our teams in Chester and Ashton-in-Makerfield regularly cover Cheshire, Greater Manchester, Merseyside, Lancashire, North Wales and the South Lake District. Wider venues, including Birmingham and the Midlands, may also be available where practical. Your venue postcode shows any additional travel charge before booking.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry public liability insurance. A copy of the certificate is available on request and can be supplied to your wedding venue when required.
