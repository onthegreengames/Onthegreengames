Frequently asked questions

Common questions, answered plainly.

How does booking work?

Use our booking builder to choose Par, Birdie, Eagle, or a Games Only package, add an Event Host if you'd like one, and see the price update live. Submit it and we'll confirm availability. A 25% deposit secures the date, with the balance due on or before the day. Until the deposit's paid, a date is provisional only.

Can I change my booking after I've paid the deposit?

Yes. Swap games, add or remove your Event Host, adjust anything, any time before the event, subject to availability. No fees for changes.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel 7 or more days before your event and you'll get a full refund of everything paid. Inside 7 days, the deposit is non-refundable, but any balance paid beyond the deposit comes back to you in full.

How long is the hire period?

There's no fixed hire period, unlike most companies, we don't build your package around a set time slot. You choose your own drop-off and pick-up times, and can have the kit all day if you like, with a latest pick-up time of 8pm.

Do you set everything up?

Yes, we deliver, set up and collect everything ourselves. There's nothing for you or your venue to arrange.

Can the mini golf be set up indoors?

Yes, the course works just as well indoors, making it a solid choice if your venue doesn't have reliable outdoor space, or as a rain backup. Some venues need floor protection or have their own restrictions on what can go inside, worth asking us about early.

What happens if it rains?

If we're set up outdoors and the weather turns, we can move the course indoors on the day where space allows, or pause and resume once it clears, rain won't cancel your entertainment. In genuinely unsafe conditions, high winds or storms, we'll talk through the options with you rather than cancelling without warning, see our full Weather Policy below.

What's included with the mini golf hire?

Putters, golf balls and scorecards are included as standard, along with delivery, professional setup and collection. No extra charges for any of it.

Can children play?

Absolutely, the course is designed to be enjoyed by guests of every age.

Do you stay and run the day, or just drop everything off?

By default, every package is self-serve, we deliver, set up and collect, and your guests play at their own pace with no fixed hire window. If you'd rather have someone running it for you, Event Host is an optional add-on available with any mini golf package: a dedicated host keeps play moving, welcomes guests to the tee, and settles friendly scoring disputes throughout your reception.

How much space do I need?

The course is modular and can be reconfigured to fit most gardens, marquees and function rooms. Send us your venue details before booking and we'll confirm it'll fit.

What if something gets damaged during the event?

Normal wear and tear is never charged. Accidental damage beyond that is charged at the rates on our damage schedule, published in full below. Minor, repairable damage is always charged at the minor rate rather than a full replacement charge.

Do you deliver outside your local area?

Yes, deliveries beyond roughly 20 miles are charged at £0.50 per mile.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry public liability insurance. A copy of the certificate is available on request, and provided automatically for venue bookings.

What information do you collect when I book?

Just what's needed to fulfil the booking: your name, contact details, event address and date. It's used only to arrange delivery, collection and communication about your booking, and isn't shared with third parties except where needed to process payment.

Rain or shine

Weather Policy

Our games are built for outdoor use and are weather resistant, but safety comes first. In light rain, games and mini golf can generally continue, and we'll relocate items undercover where space allows. Mini golf can also be set up entirely indoors on carpet or a hard floor where the venue allows it, ask us about this when booking and it removes weather as a factor completely.

In heavy rain, high winds or storm conditions, we'll adapt the layout, delay setup until it's safe, or in genuinely unsafe conditions, cancel with a full refund. We'll always talk through the options with you rather than cancelling without warning.
