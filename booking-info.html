Frequently asked questions

Common questions, answered plainly.

How does booking work?

Start by checking your date and venue postcode. If the date is available, choose a mini golf package, a games-only package or build your own combination. The site displays the package price, extras and any postcode-based travel fee before the booking is saved. A 25% deposit secures the date; until it is received, the date remains provisional.

Can I change my booking after I've paid the deposit?

Yes. Swap games, add or remove your Event Host, adjust anything at any time before the wedding, subject to availability. No fees for changes.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel 7 or more days before your wedding date and you'll get a full refund of everything paid. Inside 7 days, the deposit is non-refundable, but any balance paid beyond the deposit comes back to you in full.

How long is the hire period?

Every package is available for the full wedding day rather than a fixed two-hour or three-hour slot. Choose the delivery and collection times that work for your venue and schedule, with a latest collection time of 8pm.

Do you set everything up?

Yes. We deliver, set up and collect the mini golf course and giant garden games. The published package price includes this service, with any additional postcode-based travel fee shown separately before booking.

Can the mini golf be set up indoors?

Yes. The modular course can be arranged indoors where the venue has a suitable, reasonably flat space and permits the equipment. It can also be used in marquees, on terraces or outside on a lawn, making an indoor location a useful wet-weather option.

What happens if it rains?

If we're set up outdoors and the weather turns, we can move the course indoors on the day where space allows, or pause and resume once it clears, rain won't cancel your entertainment. In genuinely unsafe conditions, high winds or storms, we'll talk through the options with you rather than cancelling without warning, see our full Weather Policy below.

What's included with the mini golf hire?

The nine-hole course, putters, golf balls, scorecards, pencils, delivery, professional setup and collection are included. Every mini golf package is available all day, and any additional travel fee is shown from the venue postcode before booking.

Can children play?

Yes. Mini golf and the giant garden games are designed to bring children and adults together. Children should remain supervised by a responsible adult throughout the hire.

Do you stay and run the day, or just drop everything off?

Every package is self-serve as standard: we deliver, set up and collect, then your guests play throughout the day at their own pace. For mini golf packages, an Event Host can be added for £400 to welcome guests, keep play moving and manage friendly scoring disputes.

How much space do I need?

The mini golf course is modular, so the layout can be adapted for many gardens, terraces, marquees and function rooms. The space required depends on the shape of the area and the chosen layout. Provide accurate venue details and check any restrictions with the venue so suitability can be confirmed before the wedding.

What if something gets damaged during the wedding?

Normal wear and tear is never charged. If something's accidentally damaged beyond that, we'll always charge fairly based on the actual cost of repair or replacement, get in touch at onthegreengames@gmail.com if you'd like more detail before booking.

Do you deliver outside your local area?

Yes. Our teams in Chester and Ashton-in-Makerfield regularly cover Cheshire, Greater Manchester, Merseyside, Lancashire, North Wales and the South Lake District. We can also travel farther, including to Birmingham and the wider Midlands, where practical. Enter the venue postcode to see whether an additional travel charge applies.

Can I hire the giant garden games without mini golf?

Yes. Choose a games-only package with any 3, 5 or all 7 giant garden games. Games-only packages are also available for the full day and include delivery, setup and collection, plus any travel fee shown for the venue postcode.

Which areas do you cover for wedding games hire?

Our core coverage includes Cheshire, Greater Manchester, Merseyside, Lancashire, North Wales and the South Lake District, including Manchester, Liverpool, Chester, Warrington, Wigan, Stockport, Knutsford, Wilmslow, Northwich, Runcorn, Widnes, St Helens, Preston, Southport and the Wirral. Wider UK venues, including Birmingham, may also be available with an additional travel charge.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry public liability insurance. A copy of the certificate is available on request and can be supplied to your wedding venue when required.

What information do you collect when I book?

We collect the details needed to check availability and fulfil the booking, including your name, contact details, wedding date, venue, postcode and booking choices. Payment providers process payment information, and Google Analytics may collect website usage and device data as described in the Privacy Policy below.

Rain or shine

Weather Policy

Our games are built for outdoor use and are weather resistant, but safety comes first. In light rain, games and mini golf can generally continue, and we'll relocate items undercover where space allows. Mini golf can also be set up entirely indoors on carpet or a hard floor where the venue allows it, ask us about this when booking and it removes weather as a factor completely.

In heavy rain, high winds or storm conditions, we'll adapt the layout, delay setup until it's safe, or in genuinely unsafe conditions, cancel with a full refund. We'll always talk through the options with you rather than cancelling without warning.
