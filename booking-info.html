Frequently asked questions

Common questions, answered plainly.

How does booking work?

Use our booking builder to put together your day, games, mini golf, hosting, whatever combination you want, and see the price update live. Submit it and we'll confirm availability. A 25% deposit secures the date, with the balance due on or before the day. Until the deposit's paid, a date is provisional only.

Can I change my booking after I've paid the deposit?

Yes. Swap games, add or remove hosting, adjust anything, any time before the event, subject to availability. No fees for changes.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel 7 or more days before your event and you'll get a full refund of everything paid. Inside 7 days, the deposit is non-refundable, but any balance paid beyond the deposit comes back to you in full.

What time will you deliver and collect?

Whatever suits you. There's no fixed hire window, we agree delivery and collection times directly with you at booking, and it's a same-day service unless we've agreed otherwise in advance.

Can the mini golf be set up indoors?

Yes, on carpet or a hard floor where the venue allows it. This is worth asking us about early since some venues need floor protection or have their own restrictions on what can go inside.

What happens if it rains?

Giant games can be relocated undercover where space allows, and mini golf is playable in light rain. In heavy rain, high winds or storms, we'll either adapt the setup, delay until conditions improve, or, if it's genuinely unsafe, cancel with a full refund. We'll always talk it through with you rather than cancelling without warning. Indoor mini golf setup removes weather risk entirely if that's an option at your venue.

What's included with the mini golf hire?

Clubs, balls, scorecards, pencils, medals and a trophy, all included as standard. No extra charges for any of it.

Do you stay and run the day, or just drop everything off?

Both options exist. Mini Golf and standalone games are self-serve. Signature and Championship include hosting: we run the day in tournament format with an awards ceremony, for up to 8 hours. The equipment can stay with you longer than that at no extra cost, it's only the attended time that's capped.

What if something gets damaged during the event?

Normal wear and tear is never charged. Accidental damage beyond that is charged at the rates on our damage schedule, published in full below. Minor, repairable damage is always charged at the minor rate rather than a full replacement charge.

Do you deliver outside your local area?

Yes, deliveries beyond roughly 20 miles are charged at £0.50 per mile.

Are you insured?

Yes, we carry public liability insurance. A copy of the certificate is available on request, and provided automatically for venue bookings.

What information do you collect when I book?

Just what's needed to fulfil the booking: your name, contact details, event address and date. It's used only to arrange delivery, collection and communication about your booking, and isn't shared with third parties except where needed to process payment.

Rain or shine

Weather Policy

Our games are built for outdoor use and are weather resistant, but safety comes first. In light rain, games and mini golf can generally continue, and we'll relocate items undercover where space allows. Mini golf can also be set up entirely indoors on carpet or a hard floor where the venue allows it, ask us about this when booking and it removes weather as a factor completely.

In heavy rain, high winds or storm conditions, we'll adapt the layout, delay setup until it's safe, or in genuinely unsafe conditions, cancel with a full refund. We'll always talk through the options with you rather than cancelling without warning.
