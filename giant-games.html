Why wedding garden games work

Easy all-day entertainment guests can start themselves.

Giant lawn games give guests something familiar and sociable to enjoy throughout the wedding day, without needing a host, a formal start time or a short hire window.

🖼 Photo placeholder — mixed-age group playing together
Games everyone already knows

No instructions required

Jenga, Connect 4, Snakes & Ladders, noughts & crosses, all games your guests learned decades ago, just bigger. Nobody needs a rulebook or a demonstration, they can walk up and start playing straight away.

🖼 Photo placeholder — games spread across the lawn or terrace
Built for the in-between moments

Fills the natural lulls

Between the ceremony and the wedding breakfast, or while photos are being taken elsewhere, guests are often left standing around. A few games scattered across the lawn gives them somewhere to go and something to do.

Giant Connect 4

Quick, head-to-head rounds that guests can dip in and out of. Easy to play with a drink in hand, easy to stop mid-chat and pick back up.

Giant Snakes & Ladders

A genuine nostalgia hit that pulls in every generation at once. Pure luck of the dice, so grandparents beat grandkids just as often as the other way round.

🖼 Photo: Giant Noughts & Crosses

Giant Noughts & Crosses

A 30-second game guests can play on their way past, ideal for filling the smallest gaps in the day without asking for much of anyone's time.

🖼 Photo: Cornhole

Cornhole

Bean bags, boards, and a satisfying bit of competition. Works brilliantly as background entertainment while the wedding party is off having photos taken.

🖼 Photo: Giant Dominoes

Giant Dominoes

Chunky, tactile, and just as fun to stack and topple as it is to actually play. A relaxed, low-pressure option for a quieter corner of the garden.

🖼 Photo: Limbo

Limbo

Gets people up, moving, and laughing. A great energiser for the daytime drinks reception, and reliably one of the best photo and video moments of the day.
