Seven games to choose from

Familiar games guests can start themselves.

Ideal for drinks receptions, photographs and the natural gaps in the day. Guests can simply pick a game and play whenever they like.

Giant Jenga

An easy crowd-pleaser that works for all ages and builds a bit of suspense as the tower grows shakier.

Giant Connect 4

A familiar head-to-head game that guests can jump into quickly between drinks, photos and dancing.

Wooden Ring Toss

Simple, compact and easy to pick up, making it ideal for casual play throughout the day.

Giant Noughts & Crosses

A nostalgic game that adds a playful visual feature and works well for both children and adults.

Cornhole

A slightly more competitive option that’s brilliant for creating small guest tournaments and repeat play.

Giant Dominoes

A relaxed tabletop-style game that encourages slower paced play and works nicely in mixed guest groups.

Limbo

A fun, light-hearted choice that brings energy and laughter once guests are warmed up and ready to join in.
