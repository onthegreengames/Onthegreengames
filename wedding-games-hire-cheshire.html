Cheshire wedding venues

Made for Cheshire’s country houses, barns and marquees.

Plenty of Cheshire weddings happen somewhere with a lawn: country house hotels, converted barns, golf and country clubs, and marquees on farms and private estates. That’s where giant garden games come into their own, dotted around the drinks reception for guests to pick up between photos. If your day is mostly indoors, the 3-hole mini golf course fits into function rooms and marquees too.

Based in Cheshire

We’re a Cheshire business, so venues across the county are close to home for us. Enter your venue postcode when you check your date and you’ll see straight away whether a travel fee applies.

All day, not a time slot

We deliver before your guests arrive and collect at a time you choose, so the games are there from the drinks reception through to the evening do.

Lawn or indoors

The giant games are at their best on grass, and the mini golf course works on lawns, suitable terraces, in marquees and indoors where your venue allows.

Cheshire FAQs

Questions from Cheshire couples.

Do you charge a travel fee in Cheshire?

It depends on your venue’s postcode. When you check your date, enter the venue postcode and we’ll show any travel fee before you book. If your venue is within our standard area, there’s no travel fee at all.

Can you set up at a barn or marquee venue?

Yes. The giant games need a reasonably flat lawn or hard surface, and the mini golf course works on lawns, suitable terraces, in marquees and indoors where your venue permits the equipment. It’s worth checking any venue rules before you book.

Is it really hired for the whole day?

Yes. Every package is all-day hire rather than a fixed slot. You choose the delivery and collection times so the entertainment fits around your wedding.

Can you send our venue your insurance details?

Yes. We carry public liability insurance and can supply a copy of the certificate to your venue on request.
