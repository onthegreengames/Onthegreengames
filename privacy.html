Your information

Privacy & Cookie Policy

How On The Green Games uses your information and how you can control optional analytics and advertising measurement cookies.

Information we collect

We collect the information needed to check availability, calculate travel, manage and fulfil your booking. This can include your name, contact details, wedding date, venue, postcode, package choices, timings and booking notes.

Payments

Payment card information is processed by Stripe and is not stored directly by this website. We keep the booking and payment records needed to manage your booking, reconcile payments and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide the service, communicate with you about your booking, process and record payments, arrange delivery/setup/collection, prevent misuse and meet our legal obligations.

Service providers

We use service providers including Netlify and Supabase to operate the website and booking system, Stripe to process payments, Resend to send booking-related emails, and Google for optional website analytics and advertising measurement.

Cookies and similar storage

When you first visit the website, optional analytics and advertising measurement storage is denied unless you choose to allow it. We remember your cookie preference in your browser so we do not need to ask on every visit. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.

Necessary

Necessary storage supports requested website functions and remembers your cookie preference. It is not used for advertising or website analytics.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics can use cookies such as _ga to help us understand visits, popular pages and how people use the website. We use this information to improve the site.

Advertising measurement

If you allow advertising measurement, Google Ads measurement can be used to understand whether an advert click leads to a booking or another useful action on the website. This category is set up now so future Google Ads conversion tracking can respect the same choice. We do not currently use personalised advertising or remarketing through this consent setting.

Your choice

You can accept all optional cookies, reject all non-essential cookies, or allow Analytics and Advertising measurement separately. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent you from checking availability or making a booking.

How long we keep information

We retain records only for as long as they are needed for the purposes above and for any continuing legal, tax, accounting or dispute-resolution requirements.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion where there is no continuing legal reason for us to retain the information.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email bookings@onthegreengames.co.uk.
