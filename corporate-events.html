A few useful details

Questions before you book?

Can we hire the games for more than one day?

Yes. Use the slider to select up to 10 consecutive days, or send us a message about a longer event. We offer discounted rates for consecutive-day hire when the equipment stays on site.

How do I get a quote?

Choose your preferred games and hire duration, then complete the short enquiry form. We’ll check the event details, availability and travel arrangements before sending a tailored quote.

Do you deliver and collect?

Yes, we arrange delivery, setup and collection with you. Any applicable travel fee and agreed access arrangements will be confirmed before you book.

Can we hire mini golf without the garden games?

Absolutely. You can hire our three-hole mini golf course on its own, or add one, two or three holes to a garden games collection.

Can you provide staff during the event?

Tell us what you need when you enquire. Standard hire is unstaffed; if you need attendance or additional visits, we'll discuss feasibility and quote separately.
