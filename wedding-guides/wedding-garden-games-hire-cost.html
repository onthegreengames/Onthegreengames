Costs and prices

How Much Does It Cost to Hire Garden Games for a Wedding?

Wedding garden games hire can cost a few hundred pounds, but the headline price doesn't tell you everything. I'd check how many games you're getting, how long they stay at the venue and whether the supplier handles delivery and collection.

By Jordan & Dayle · 3 November 2026 · 3 min read

Rather than guessing a national average, I'll show you how our pricing works and the questions I'd ask anyone supplying wedding games.

What changes the price?

The number and type of games is the obvious starting point. A collection of smaller wooden games may cost less than one that includes larger pieces. Suppliers also price their time differently. Some quote for a couple of hours, while others provide equipment for the whole day.

Travel can make a difference too. A low headline price may look less attractive once delivery, setup and collection are added. Before comparing companies, ask what's actually included.

Our current garden games prices

We have seven games and four ready-made collections. At our standard advertised prices, the Classic Collection costs £200 for all four Classic games: Jenga, Wooden Ring Toss, Giant Dominoes and Limbo.

The Mix costs £325 for two Premium and two Classic games. The Premium Collection costs £350 for all three Premium games: Giant Connect 4, Giant Noughts & Crosses and Cornhole. The Complete Collection costs £475 for all seven.

You can also build your own selection, with Classic games at £50 each and Premium games at £125 each, subject to the £300 Build Your Own minimum. Fixed collections aren't subject to that minimum.

These figures are our standard prices as at November 2026, not a promise they'll stay the same. Check the current wedding games packages page before making a decision.

What does that price include?

Our collections include all-day hire, delivery, setup and collection. We agree practical drop-off and collection times around your plans, rather than charging for a short fixed slot.

Any applicable travel fee depends on your venue postcode and is shown automatically on the booking page before you pay. Temporary offers, when available, can change the final amount, so the booking summary is the figure to use.

Is all-day hire worth having?

Yes, and not just because it fills gaps in the afternoon. With a shorter hire, the games may be collected before the evening reception begins, so anyone arriving later misses them completely. All-day hire means evening guests can enjoy them too, rather than only the people who were there for the drinks reception.

We also like having the games available later on. Not everyone wants to spend the whole evening on the dance floor, and a quick game of Connect 4 or Jenga gives people a chance to step away, have a laugh and head back inside. We include all-day hire as standard and agree collection times around your plans, as long as the venue can keep the space available and it's safe to play into the evening.

What if you want mini golf too?

A portable mini golf course can work alongside garden games, but I'd compare combined prices rather than assume separate package totals always apply. We offer one, two or three mini golf holes alongside games, with a standalone three-hole package also available.

Think about whether you'll have room for both activities and whether guests will have time to use them. Booking more equipment doesn't automatically make the entertainment better.

Five questions for any hire company

Exactly which games are included? How long can guests use them? Who delivers and sets them up? Are travel charges included or separate? What happens if you need to make changes?

Those answers are more useful than comparing prices in isolation. I'd choose the setup that works with your venue and guests, and check the final quote before paying.
