Nine-Hole Wedding Mini Golf, Delivered and Set Up For You

Designed specifically for weddings, our nine-hole course works equally well in a marquee, a function room or out on the lawn. It gives guests something genuinely fun to do throughout the day, while you focus on celebrating rather than entertaining.

Built to fit your venue

Gardens, terraces, or indoors

The course is modular, so it adapts to gardens, hard-standing terraces and indoor spaces alike, and it doubles as a ready-made wet-weather backup if you're planning to be outdoors.

On your schedule, not ours

No fixed hire window
