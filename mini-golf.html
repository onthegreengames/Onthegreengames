Wedding mini golf FAQs

A few questions about hiring our mini golf.

Can we hire mini golf without any garden games?

Yes. Our standalone 3-hole wedding mini golf package is £275, with putters, balls, scorecards, delivery, setup and collection included. It is all-day hire rather than a fixed time slot.

Can we add mini golf to a garden games package?

Yes. Choose one, two or all three mini golf holes alongside a garden games collection or Build Your Own package. The booking page shows the combined price and any current offer before you pay.

Can the mini golf course go indoors or in a marquee?

Yes, where your venue allows the equipment and has a suitable, reasonably flat area. The three separate holes can be positioned to make the best use of the space available.

Do you deliver wedding crazy golf throughout the North West?

We cover Cheshire, Merseyside, Greater Manchester, Lancashire, North Wales and the South Lake District, with wider venues available where practical. Check your venue postcode on the booking page to see coverage and any travel fee.
