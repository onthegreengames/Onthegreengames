Manchester wedding venues

From city-centre mills to the edge of the Pennines.

Greater Manchester has a real mix of wedding venues: converted mills and warehouses, city hotels, sports grounds, and country houses and golf clubs out towards Cheshire and the Pennine hills. Indoor venues suit the compact mini golf course, while anywhere with a garden or terrace works well for the giant games.

The three mini golf holes can be arranged around the space available rather than needing one fixed footprint, which helps in function rooms and smaller city venues.

Greater Manchester FAQs

Questions from Greater Manchester couples.

Do you charge a travel fee in Greater Manchester?

It depends on your venue’s postcode. Enter it when you check your date and any travel fee is shown before you pay. If your venue is within our standard area, there’s no travel fee.

Can you set up in a mill or warehouse venue?

Yes, as long as there’s a reasonably flat area and the venue permits the equipment. The mini golf course and most of the giant games work well indoors.

Can we change our package after booking?

Yes. You can add or remove games or mini-golf holes before the wedding, subject to availability, and we don’t charge amendment fees. You just pay any difference in price.

Can children play?

Yes. The mini golf and giant garden games suit mixed-age wedding groups. Children should be supervised by a responsible adult throughout the hire.
