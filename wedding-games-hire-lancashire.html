Lancashire wedding venues

Made for Lancashire’s barns, estates and outdoor weddings.

Lancashire is known for its rural wedding venues: barns and country estates in the Ribble Valley and around the Forest of Bowland, and hotels and gardens along the Fylde coast at Lytham St Annes. Many of these have the lawns and courtyards that giant garden games are made for, and the mini golf course can be set up in a marquee or indoors if you’d rather.

Room to spread out

Giant Jenga, Connect 4, cornhole and the rest spread out nicely across a lawn or courtyard, so guests can drift between them all day.

Collected when you choose

You pick the collection time. For longer journeys we may occasionally need to collect a little earlier than requested, though this is rare.

Travel fee shown up front

Enter your venue postcode when you check your date and you’ll see any travel fee before you pay, from Preston to the Ribble Valley and the coast.

Lancashire FAQs

Questions from Lancashire couples.

Do you cover the Ribble Valley and Lancaster?

Yes, Lancashire is within the areas we cover. Enter your venue postcode when you check your date to see availability and any travel fee before you book.

Do the games work on uneven ground?

The games and mini golf need a suitable, reasonably flat area. A lawn, courtyard or terrace is ideal, and it’s worth checking with your venue where they’d be happy for everything to go.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn’t automatically stop the entertainment. Where practical we can use an indoor or covered setup, and in genuinely unsafe weather we’ll talk you through the options rather than cancelling without warning.

Is it really hired for the whole day?

Yes. Every package is all-day hire rather than a fixed slot, with delivery, setup and collection included.
