Where we cover

Wedding game hire around South Cumbria.

Our South Lake District coverage includes Kendal, Windermere, Bowness-on-Windermere, Cartmel and Grange-over-Sands. If you're planning a wedding elsewhere in Cumbria, get in touch and we'll check whether we can make the journey.

All-day hire

We deliver and set up around your wedding schedule, and you can choose the collection time. Longer-distance arrangements are confirmed around what works for your venue.

Space that suits the games

Garden games are at their best on a reasonably flat outdoor surface. The compact mini golf course can also work indoors or under cover where space and venue rules allow.

Travel cost upfront

Enter your wedding venue's postcode when you check the date. Any applicable travel fee will be shown before you pay. For postcodes not recognised, we'll confirm availability and pricing manually.

South Lakes FAQs

A few things worth checking with your venue.

Do you cover all of Cumbria?

Our regular coverage focuses on the South Lakes. We can consider other Cumbria venues depending on the date, distance and collection arrangements. Contact us if your venue is outside the areas listed above.

What if our venue doesn't have a big lawn?

Our giant games can be spread out or grouped together on suitable flat ground. The three mini golf holes are compact and can be set up indoors or under cover where your venue permits it. Check with us and your venue if space is limited.

Can you show a delivery or travel charge before we book?

Yes. Enter your venue's postcode when checking your date and you'll see any available travel fee alongside the hire price before payment. If your postcode needs a manual quote, email us and we'll confirm it.
